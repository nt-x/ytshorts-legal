YTShortsGen

YTShortsGen is a private tool used by a single creator to produce short educational videos (technology, history, mathematics and geography) and upload them to the creator's own YouTube channels.

The application uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos, set their titles, descriptions and captions, on channels owned by the operator. It is not offered to the public and does not access other users' accounts.
